Mei is sorting her family's photos, and her photo catalog describes every one of them, about
seventy thousand so far. Every hour of the day and night, a program on her laptop copies any new
photos from the family's three phones into a folder. Then the tagging agent, following a skill file
Mei wrote, looks at each photo copied in that hour, decides who is in it and what the occasion was,
and records the photo in the photo catalog with those tags and the date it was taken. Most runs
take about ten minutes. After a birthday party or a holiday weekend, though, a run can take two
hours. Each December the calendar program picks out only that year's photos tagged with both of
Mei's grandchildren and lays out a family calendar from them; it never changes or adds anything in
the photo catalog.
