Lena cooks most nights and keeps a list of recipes she might try, which now holds about
twenty-two thousand entries. Every two hours from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m., a program on her laptop saves
the new posts from eight food blogs she follows into a folder. Then the recipe agent, following a
skill file Lena wrote, reads each new post, decides whether the dish suits her diet and her
kitchen, and adds an entry at the end of the recipe list for each one worth keeping, with its
cooking time and main ingredients. A run takes about fifteen minutes. Most evenings, often while a
run is going, Lena looks over that day's new entries at the end of the list, fixes any cooking
time the agent got wrong, and marks the dishes she wants to make 'this week'. Each Sunday
afternoon the planning program picks out only the entries marked 'this week' and makes her a
grocery list from them; it never changes or adds anything in the recipe list.
