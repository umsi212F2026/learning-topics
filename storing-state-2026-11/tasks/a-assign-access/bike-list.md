Omar is looking for a used road bike in his size and keeps a list of bikes worth a look, which
holds about sixty entries. Every hour from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m., a program on his laptop saves the new
listings from the bicycle section of the local classified ads site into a folder. Then the bike
agent, following a skill file Omar wrote, reads each new listing, decides whether the bike fits his
size and budget, and adds an entry to the bike list for each one worth a look, with its frame size,
asking price and the seller's town. A run takes about ten minutes. Most days after dinner, from
about 7:30 until about nine, Omar goes down the bike list from the top, changes an entry's status
to 'messaged' when he writes to the seller, and changes it to 'gone' when a seller tells him the
bike has sold. Every Friday at noon the summary program goes over the whole bike list and makes
Omar a one-page table of every bike on it, cheapest first; it never changes or adds anything in
the bike list.
