Noor follows about a hundred podcasts and keeps a listening list with an entry for every episode
they have released since she started it five years ago, about twenty-five thousand entries now.
Each night at 2 a.m., the episode agent, following a skill file Noor wrote, checks her podcasts for
new episodes and adds an entry to the listening list for each one, with its length, a one-line
summary and a guess at whether she will like it; it never changes an entry already there. Its run
is done by 2:30. Noor chooses what to play by looking over the newest entries at the top of the
list. After each episode she records her rating out of five in its entry, always between about
7 a.m. and 11 p.m. An entry shows one rating, her latest, since she sometimes changes her mind on
a second listen. Every Friday at 6 p.m., the playlist program picks out only that week's episodes
she rated four or five and makes a playlist to send to her sister; it never changes or adds
anything in the listening list.
