# Single source of truth

### vs-backup

A youth soccer league keeps its team rosters in a spreadsheet that the registrar edits. When a
player's phone number changes, the registrar changes it there, and the coaches' contact lists are
printed from the spreadsheet again. Every Friday night, a copy of the spreadsheet is saved to an
external drive, which nobody opens unless the spreadsheet itself is lost.

#### a

Which term best describes the copy on the external drive?

1. A single source of truth
2. A backup
3. Neither

#### b

Which term best describes the registrar's spreadsheet?

1. A single source of truth
2. A backup
3. Neither

### catch-pick

A neighborhood food pantry keeps its volunteer shift schedule in one spreadsheet, which only the
coordinator edits. Every Monday, the sign-up sheet posted at the door is printed from it. Each
night, a copy of the spreadsheet is saved to a second drive in case the first one fails.

Which of these uses the term single source of truth correctly?

1. The door sheet is the single source of truth, since it is what volunteers read.
2. The coordinator's spreadsheet is the single source of truth for the shifts.
3. Printing the door sheet means the pantry no longer has a single source of truth.
4. With a single source of truth, the pantry has no need for the nightly copy.

### catch-copy

A tutoring center keeps its students' session times in one spreadsheet that the front desk edits.
Every Sunday night, a copy of the spreadsheet is saved to an external drive. On Wednesday, the
front desk moved one student's session from Tuesday to Thursday in the spreadsheet. A tutor who
isn't sure which day is right says:

"Check the copy on the external drive. That's our single source of truth."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.
