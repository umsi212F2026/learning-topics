Steps 2 and 4 are the lumped ones. Step 2 is `handoff-split`: the agent's labels can be checked
against the landlord's written rules and the requests, while the landlord's choosing and ordering
are judgment calls, checked by someone who knows the buildings and the tenants. Its join with no
change is between choosing and ordering, both the landlord's judgment. Step 4 is `check-split`:
the same agent does all three parts. Matching tenants to bookings and listing the visits by day
are both checked against the bookings from step 3 (every booking appears once, at the right day
and hour), so there is no boundary between them; the texts need a person to read them for tone
and clarity, so the boundary goes before the texts.
