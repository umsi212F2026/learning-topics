Steps 2 and 3 are the lumped ones. Step 2 is `handoff-split`: the agent's suggested prices can be
checked against what similar bikes recently sold for, while the owner's final prices and front-page
picks are judgment calls, checked by someone who knows the shop and its buyers. Its join with no
change is between the owner's two decisions. Step 3 is `check-split`: the same agent does all three
parts. Filling in the fields and marking each listing shippable or pickup only are both checked
against the inventory list, the final prices and the shop's size rule, so there is no boundary
between them, though they produce things that look different. The descriptions need a person to
read them for honesty and clarity, so the boundary goes before the descriptions.
