# Simulation

### catch-rope-off

A science museum wants to know whether a one-way route through its dinosaur galleries would stop
visitors crowding into the first room. One of the staff says:

"Let's simulate the one-way route. Next Saturday we'll rope off the galleries so everyone has to
follow it, and count how many visitors are in each room every fifteen minutes."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### vs-experiment

An airline wants to know whether boarding a plane from the back rows forward gets everyone seated
faster than boarding in random order. It could find out with a simulation or with an experiment.
What is the difference between the two, here? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A warehouse wants to know whether adding a third loading dock would cut how long delivery trucks
wait to be unloaded.

Which of these statements uses the word "simulation" correctly?

1. Having a computer play out many days of trucks under rules for three docks would be a simulation.
2. Averaging how long trucks waited in last year's logs, with two docks, would be a simulation.
3. Working out the wait at three docks from a waiting-line formula on paper would be a simulation.
4. Asking drivers how long they expect to wait once there are three docks would be a simulation.
