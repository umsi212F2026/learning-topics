# Simulation model

### catch-steady-wind

A county's fire team has a simulation model of how a wildfire spreads across a stretch of forest.
To keep it simple, the model's rules hold the wind at one steady speed and direction for the whole
afternoon. During a real fire, one of the team says:

"Our simulation model says the wind stays steady all afternoon, so we don't need anyone watching
for gusts."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### vs-real-process

A company that runs the elevators in a 30-floor office tower has a simulation model of its morning
rush. What is the difference between that simulation model and the real process it describes?
Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A concert hall is planning how its audience leaves after a show, and wants a simulation model of
the hall emptying.

Which of these statements uses the words "simulation model" correctly?

1. The architect's cardboard scale model of the hall, with its doors and aisles, is the simulation model.
2. The fire code, saying how many exits the hall needs and how wide, is the simulation model.
3. "Most people head for the door they came in by" is a simulation model of the hall, as it stands.
4. The rules a computer follows for which exit each person takes, and how fast, are the simulation model.
