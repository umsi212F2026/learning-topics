# Simulation trial

### vs-different-parameters

A marathon's organizers have a simulation model of race day at the water stations. Runners reach
each station at moments drawn at random, and one trial records the longest line at any station.
They have run the model 2,000 times with 12 water stations along the course. Now they run it again
with 15 stations.

What is the difference between one more trial with 12 stations and a run with 15 stations? Answer
in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-each-roll

A family has a simulation model of a board game in which players take turns rolling a die and
moving along a track of 100 squares, with shortcuts that jump a player ahead and slides that send
a player back. They want to know how many turns a game usually lasts. One of them says:

"Each roll of the die is one simulation trial, so a game that lasts 40 turns is 40 trials."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A call center has a simulation model of one weekday: calls arrive at random moments, a set number
of agents answer them, and the model records the longest anyone was on hold.

Which of these statements uses the words "simulation trial" correctly?

1. Running the model 500 times with 8 agents is one simulation trial of the 8-agent setting.
2. After 500 simulated days with 8 agents, a day with 9 agents is a 501st simulation trial of the 8-agent setting.
3. One simulated weekday with 8 agents, its calls drawn at random afresh, is a simulation trial.
4. Putting 9 agents on the real phones for a week to test the change is a simulation trial.
