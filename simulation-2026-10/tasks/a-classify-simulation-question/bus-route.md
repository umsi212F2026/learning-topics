This made-up simulation models one weekday on a city bus route: buses leave the depot on a
timetable, stop to pick up and drop off riders, and fall behind when traffic is slow. It leaves out
breakdowns: no bus in the model stops running partway through the day. Its model parameters are
the number of buses on the route, the minutes between departures from the depot, and the number of
riders who arrive at each stop each hour. One trial is one simulated weekday, and it records how
long each rider waited, how late each bus reached each stop, and how many riders were left behind
at a stop because the bus was full. Chance is built into its rules: riders turn up at random
moments, at the rate the third parameter sets, and the time a bus takes between stops varies at
random with the traffic.

Each question below comes from a different person who wants this simulation to answer one
question. Each question is about this simulation alone.

full. How many buses would the route need for that to happen to anyone on no more than 1 weekday
in 25?"
