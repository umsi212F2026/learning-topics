This made-up simulation models a city's bike-share system over one weekday: riders take bikes
from docking stations and leave them at others, and a van moves bikes from full stations to empty
ones. It leaves out broken bikes: every bike in the model can always be ridden. Its model
parameters are the number of docks at each station, the number of bikes the van moves each hour,
and the number of riders who arrive at each station each hour. One trial is one simulated weekday,
and it records, for each station, how many minutes it sat with no bikes and how many minutes it
sat with no free dock. Chance is built into its rules: riders turn up at random moments, at the
rate the third parameter sets, and each one picks a destination station at random.

Each question below comes from a different person who wants this simulation to answer one
question. Each question is about this simulation alone.
