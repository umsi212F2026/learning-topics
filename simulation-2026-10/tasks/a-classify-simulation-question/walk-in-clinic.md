This made-up simulation models one day in a walk-in clinic: patients arrive, wait in the waiting
room for a free exam room and a free doctor, are seen, and leave. It leaves out paperwork: a visit
ends the moment the doctor finishes the exam. Its model parameters are the number of doctors on
shift, the number of exam rooms, and the number of patients who walk in each hour. One trial is
one simulated day, and it records how long each patient waited, how many were still waiting at
closing, and how many minutes each doctor spent with no patient. Chance is built into its rules:
patients walk in at random moments, at the rate the third parameter sets, and each visit takes a
random number of minutes.

Each question below comes from a different person who wants this simulation to answer one
question. Each question is about this simulation alone.
