This made-up simulation models a flu outbreak at one school over a term: one sick student arrives,
and the flu spreads from student to student in classrooms until no one is left who is sick. It
leaves out teachers and staff: only students in the model catch or spread the flu. Its model
parameters are the number of students who have had a flu shot, the number of days a sick student
stays home, and the number of other students each student sits near in class. One trial is one
simulated outbreak, and it records how many students got sick, how many were out sick each day,
and how many days the outbreak lasted. Chance is built into its rules: each school day, a sick
student passes the flu to each student sitting near them with some chance, which is smaller for a
student who has had the shot.

Each question below comes from a different person who wants this simulation to answer one
question. Each question is about this simulation alone.
