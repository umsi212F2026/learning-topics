This made-up model follows a chairlift at a ski resort over one day: skiers line up at the bottom, ride to the top on chairs that leave at a fixed spacing, and ski back down to line up again. It leaves out lift stops: the lift never pauses for a fallen rider or for high wind. Its model parameters are the number of skiers on the mountain, the number of seats on each chair, the time between chairs, and the average time a skier takes to ski back down. One simulation trial is one day, from the lift's opening to its closing, and records how long each skier waits in line for each ride and how many skiers are still in line when the lift stops loading. Each time a skier sets off down the mountain, the time the run takes is drawn at random around the average set by the last parameter.

Each question below comes from a different person who wants this simulation to answer one question. Each question is about this simulation alone.
