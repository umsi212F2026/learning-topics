A stochastic model of one year of a river town's flooding, with three model parameters (the
levee's height, storms each spring, water the pumps clear each hour) and three outcomes per trial
(the river's highest level, days water stood in the low streets, which houses took water). The
setup gives no parameter values. What it leaves out, the riverbanks wearing away, is something no
question asks about. Shapes in slot order: `threshold-ahead`, `likely-ahead`, `past-chance`,
`threshold-worst`. Between them the four questions carry four of the five cases, all but `can-happen`.

### q1

- **goal:** `c-name-question-kind`
- **cases:** target-setting, what-if-ahead, chance-in-it
- **type:** mcq
- **answer:** 4
- **credit:** full for 4 only.
- **tutor note:** Elena asks for the value of one model parameter, the levee's height, at which an outcome (dry low streets) holds in at least 49 years out of 50. Her "What if" is about a rebuild still to come, and the frequency phrase says how often the target must hold. A learner who picks 1 has read "What if" as a question about the past; ask whether the levee has been rebuilt yet. One who picks 2 has followed "49 years out of 50"; ask whether Elena wants back a frequency or a height.
