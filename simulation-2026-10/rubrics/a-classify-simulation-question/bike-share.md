A stochastic model of one weekday of a city's bike-share system, with three model parameters
(docks at each station, bikes the van moves each hour, riders arriving at each station each hour)
and two outcomes per station per trial (minutes with no bikes, minutes with no free dock). The
setup gives no parameter values. What it leaves out, broken bikes, is something no question asks
about. Shapes in slot order: `better-ahead`, `threshold-ahead`, `possible-worst`,
`how-bad-chance`. Between them the four questions carry all five cases.

### q1

- **goal:** `c-name-question-kind`
- **cases:** what-if-ahead
- **type:** mcq
- **answer:** 4
- **credit:** full for 4 only.
- **tutor note:** Priya names two options still to be chosen, each a change to a model parameter (the van's bikes each hour, the docks at each station), and asks which comes out ahead on minutes empty or full. Her "What if" is about a choice ahead, not a replay of the past. A learner who picks 1 has read "What if" as a question about the past; ask whether either change has happened yet.
