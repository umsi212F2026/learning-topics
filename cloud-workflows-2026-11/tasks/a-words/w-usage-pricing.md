# Usage-based pricing

### vs-subscription

A translation service offers two plans. Plan A charges 2 cents for each page translated, with no monthly fee. Plan B charges $15 each month, and the user may translate as many pages as they like up to 2,000 a month.

Which plan is usage-based pricing?

1. Plan A
2. Plan B
3. Both
4. Neither

### catch-pick

A model company bills its API at a set price for each million tokens sent and returned, with no monthly fee and no monthly limit. Last month a club's workflow ran twice as often as the month before, and its bill doubled.

Which of these uses the term usage-based pricing correctly?

1. Under usage-based pricing, the bill doubled because the club went past a monthly limit.
2. Under usage-based pricing, the club pays the same each month however often it runs.
3. Under usage-based pricing, the price of each token rose as the club used more.
4. Under usage-based pricing, twice as many runs led to about twice the bill.
