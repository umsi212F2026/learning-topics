Kofi built a workflow on his laptop for a group of people who bike to work downtown. A member types in their home address, their workplace and the hours they ride, and an agent following Kofi's skill file drafts that member's safest route.

The group has about forty members, and each asks for a route when they move or change jobs. Every member has a laptop and has paid for a subscription to a model company's chat app for years, and each has switched off the setting that lets the company train its models on their chats.

When Kofi tried an open-weight model on his own commute, its routes often used busy roads.

Plan A: Each member runs the workflow on their own laptop, with the agent step working under their own subscription to a model company's chat app.

Plan B: Kofi runs the workflow as a web service, BYOK: each member gives it their own API key, and the agent step's model calls are billed to that key.
