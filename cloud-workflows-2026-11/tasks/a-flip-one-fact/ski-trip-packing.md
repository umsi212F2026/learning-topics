Marco built a workflow on his laptop for his ski club: an agent, following his skill file, drafts a packing list for one weekend trip. It works only from the resort's published snow forecast and the club's posted trip calendar.

The club has about seventy members, and most go on four or five trips a winter. Every member has a laptop and a chat subscription. Each has switched off the setting in it that lets the model company train on their chats.

Plan A: The club runs the workflow as a web service, BYOK: each member gives it their own API key, and the agent step's model calls are billed to that key.

Plan B: Each member runs the workflow on their own laptop, with the agent step working under their own subscription to a model company's chat app.
