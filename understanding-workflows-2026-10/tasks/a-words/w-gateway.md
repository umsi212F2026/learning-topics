# Gateway

### vs-step

A print shop has drawn its order workflow. The prepress technician does "Check the file". Next
comes a diamond labeled "File print-ready?": "yes" leads straight to "Print the job", and "no"
leads to "Fix the file". Just before "Print the job", an unlabeled diamond joins the two paths
again.

In this diagram, what is the difference between a gateway and a workflow step? Answer in two or
three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-check

An accounts office has drawn its workflow for paying supplier invoices. A new clerk, describing the
diagram, says:

"The gateway 'Invoice matches order?' is where I compare the invoice against the purchase order,
line by line, and note any differences."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A university office has drawn its workflow for booking a meeting room. A staff member does "Check
the calendar". Then a diamond labeled "Room free?" sends the run to "Confirm the booking" on yes,
or "Offer another room" on no. An unlabeled diamond joins the two paths again just before "Send
confirmation email".

Which of these statements uses the word "gateway" correctly?

1. "Room free?" is a gateway, so it is where the staff member looks through the calendar.
2. "Check the calendar" is a gateway, since what it finds decides which way the run goes.
3. The unlabeled diamond is no gateway, since nothing is decided there and no question asked.
4. The unlabeled diamond is a gateway too: it is where the two paths come back together.
