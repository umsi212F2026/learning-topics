# Lane

### catch-phase

A restaurant has drawn its workflow for serving a table, with three lanes: Server, Kitchen, and
Cashier. The server takes each table's order and passes it to the kitchen. A new manager,
redrawing the diagram, says:

"The Kitchen lane is the food part of the meal, so 'Take the order' belongs in it, since an order
is all about food."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A student club has drawn its workflow for paying back members who spend their own money on club
supplies. It has three lanes, Member, Treasurer, and Bank. The step "Approve request" sits in the
Treasurer lane, and the step "Pay out" is drawn across both the Treasurer and Bank lanes.

Which of these statements uses the word "lane" correctly?

1. The Treasurer lane is the treasurer's job description, listing all she does for the club.
2. "Approve request" sits in the Treasurer lane, so the treasurer is the one who does it.
3. The lanes are stages in time: the Member lane comes first, then Treasurer, then Bank.
4. "Pay out" spans two lanes, so it happens twice in each run, once in each of the lanes.
