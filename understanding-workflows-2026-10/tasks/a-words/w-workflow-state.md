# Workflow state

### vs-step

A dental office has drawn its appointment workflow. In order, the diagram shows "Appointment
booked", "Send a reminder text", "Appointment confirmed", "Clean the patient's teeth", and "Visit
complete".

In this diagram, what is the difference between a workflow state and a workflow step? Answer in
two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A bike shop has drawn its repair workflow, with two lanes, Front desk and Mechanic. It starts from
"Bike dropped off". The mechanic does "Inspect the bike", then the flow divides at "Repair
needed?", and either path ends at "Bike ready for pickup".

Which of these statements uses the words "workflow state" correctly?

1. "Inspect the bike" is a workflow state, since the mechanic spends a while in it.
2. "Repair needed?" is a workflow state, since it is where the flow divides in two.
3. The Mechanic lane is a workflow state, since the mechanic is in it for the whole run.
4. "Bike ready for pickup" is a workflow state, since it holds until the owner comes.

### catch-call

A veterinary clinic has drawn its surgery-day workflow. In order, the diagram shows "Patient
checked in", "Give anesthesia", "Patient in recovery", "Call the owner", and "Patient discharged".
A vet tech, explaining the diagram, says:

"'Call the owner' is a workflow state: it's the state the run is in while the receptionist is on
the phone."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.
