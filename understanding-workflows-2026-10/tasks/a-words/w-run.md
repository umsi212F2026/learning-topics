# Run

### vs-workflow

A dog shelter has drawn its adoption process as a diagram: an application arrives, a staff member
checks the references, a volunteer makes a home visit, and the adoption is either signed or
declined. Last month the shelter handled fourteen applications this way, among them the Lopez
family's application for a beagle named Rosie, which was signed on May 3.

In this situation, what is the difference between a run and the workflow? Answer in two or three
sentences, in your own words.

### catch-name

The coordinator of a tutoring program says:

"Our run for onboarding new tutors has five steps. I drew it up in 2024, and we've used it for
every new tutor since."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A bike rental shop has a rental workflow: the customer signs a waiver, a staff member fits a bike,
the customer rides and brings it back, and a staff member inspects it. Last Saturday the shop
rented out thirty-two bikes, among them one to Maria, who rode from 9 a.m. to noon.

Which of these statements uses the word "run" correctly?

1. The shop changed its run last spring, adding the waiver for every customer from then on.
2. The run is the part where the staff member inspects the returned bike for any damage.
3. Last Saturday was one run of the rental workflow, with thirty-two customers in it.
4. Maria's rental was one run, from signing her waiver to her bike's inspection at noon.
