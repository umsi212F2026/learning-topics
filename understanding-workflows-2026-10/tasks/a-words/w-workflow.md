# Workflow

### vs-project

A neighborhood food pantry does two kinds of things. Every Saturday it takes in new clients: a
greeter welcomes each one, the desk volunteer registers them, and a shopper volunteer walks them
through the shelves, in that order, until the client leaves with a bag of groceries. This spring the
pantry is also moving to a bigger building, which nobody there has done before, and the board is
working out what to do next as it goes.

In this situation, what is the difference between a workflow and a project? Answer in two or three
sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A community theater puts on four shows a year, with a volunteer cast and crew.

Which of these statements uses the word "workflow" correctly?

1. Its workflow for this spring's show, the one time it stages that play, is planned month by month.
2. Its workflow for closing up after a show is a list: lights, doors, props, done in any order.
3. Its workflow for casting is open auditions, then callbacks by the director, then offers by email.
4. Its workflow is a full house on opening night, the result every one of its shows works toward.

### vs-checklist

A summer camp has two written procedures for its last day. One lists what a counselor does before
leaving a cabin: strip the beds, sweep the floor, check under the bunks for left-behind items, and
turn in the key, in whatever order suits the counselor. The other covers sending campers home: a
parent shows ID at the gate, the office checks the ID against the pickup list and radios the
cabin, and then the counselor walks the camper to the gate.

In this situation, what is the difference between a workflow and a checklist? Answer in two or
three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-garcia

A print shop handles every customer order the same way: the front desk takes the order, the
designer prepares the file, and the press operator prints it. The shop manager says:

"Yesterday's workflow for the Garcia order took four hours, because the printer jammed twice."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.
