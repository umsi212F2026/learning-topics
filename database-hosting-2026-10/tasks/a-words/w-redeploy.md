### q-redeploy-vs-restart

In Problem Set 2 you restarted your local server to check that your data was still there. On a
host, your agent talks about redeploying your app. What is the difference between a restart and a
redeploy?

### q-interpret-redeploy

Your app is already running on a host. Your agent says: "I've fixed the typo on the sign-up page
and pushed it. The host will redeploy in about a minute." What is the agent claiming, and what does
it rule out?
