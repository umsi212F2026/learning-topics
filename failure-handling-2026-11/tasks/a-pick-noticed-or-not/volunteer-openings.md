Grace keeps a list of volunteer openings she might sign up for, drawn from the newsletters of community groups in her town.

- Every Saturday at 6 a.m. a saving program saves the page of each volunteer opening the week's newsletters link to into a folder.
- Then the assessing agent, following a skill file Grace wrote, reads each saved page and adds an entry for it to the openings list, rated 'good fit' or 'not for me' against the hours she has free and the kinds of work she enjoys; an opening whose page wasn't saved gets no entry.
- Then the details program pulls the date, the start time and the meeting place out of each saved page and writes them into that opening's entry.
- Grace opens the laptop on Saturday afternoon, reads every entry in the openings list, 'good fit' and 'not for me' alike, and adds the shifts she wants to her calendar.

This Saturday one group's site didn't answer when the saving program asked for an opening's page, so the program wrote a line saying so to a problems file in the workflow's folder and went on to the next opening.
