Hector wants cheap used copies of the five textbooks for his spring classes.

- Every evening at 7 p.m. a saving program saves the page of each used copy of those books offered on two bookseller sites into the textbooks folder.
- Then the assessing agent, following a skill file Hector wrote, reads each saved page and adds an entry for that copy to the textbooks list, rated 'buy', 'maybe' or 'skip' against the edition his classes use and the condition he'll accept.
- Then the details program pulls each copy's price and shipping cost out of its saved page and writes them into that copy's entry.
- Then a comparing program compares each entry's price with the price on its saved page. It puts any entry where the two differ at the top of the textbooks list.
- Each night Hector reads the textbooks list from the top and orders the copies he wants.

On Monday the details program wrote one copy's shipping cost, $4, as its price; its saved page gives the price as $38.
