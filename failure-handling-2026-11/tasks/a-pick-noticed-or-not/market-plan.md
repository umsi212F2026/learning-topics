Wendell plans his Sunday shopping at his town's farmers market.

- Each Friday at noon a saving program saves the market's page for the coming Sunday into the market folder. That page lists each vendor with the items it will bring and their prices.
- Then the planning agent, following a skill file Wendell wrote, reads the saved page and adds an entry to the Sunday list for each item on his weekly shopping list, naming the vendor that sells it for the least.
- On Sunday morning Wendell takes the Sunday list to the market and buys each item from the vendor its entry names.

This week the agent named the bakery stall for rye bread, though the saved page shows rye bread at another stall for $2 less.
