# Fail loudly

### vs-fail-fast

Two steps from Rosa's photo workflow.

Each night, a step copies the day's photos from her phone to a backup drive. When a photo won't
copy, the step stops right there, leaving the rest of the day's photos uncopied, and writes the
reason to a log file on the drive. Rosa has never opened that file.

Each morning, a step has an agent write a caption for each photo backed up the night before. When a
photo can't be read, the step skips it, captions the rest, and adds the skipped photo's name to a
short list that is emailed to Rosa. She reads that email every morning.

#### a

Which best describes the copying step?

1. Both
2. Fails loudly, but not fast
3. Fails fast, but not loudly
4. Neither

#### b

Which best describes the captioning step?

1. Both
2. Fails loudly, but not fast
3. Fails fast, but not loudly
4. Neither

### catch-pick

Each night, a step reads the day's runs from Sam's watch export and adds them to his training sheet.
When one run's record is damaged, the step stops at once, so the rest of that night's runs are not
added, and it writes "bad record" in a file in a folder Sam never opens.

Which of these uses the term fail loudly correctly?

1. Since the step stops the moment it meets one, it fails loudly.
2. It doesn't fail loudly, because nobody ever sees the note it writes.
3. It fails loudly, since it writes a note for every bad record it meets.
4. To fail loudly a step must stop; carrying on would hide the problem.

### catch-red-warning

A hiking club matches members' payments to their names each night. The step runs at 2 a.m. on a
computer in the club's storage closet that nobody logs into. When a payment can't be matched, the
step shows a large red warning on that computer's screen and goes on to the next payment. The
treasurer says:

"That step fails loudly. Nobody could miss a warning like that."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.
