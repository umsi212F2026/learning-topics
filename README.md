# SI 212 — learning topics

**This is where your learning work lives.** One folder per topic, each holding what you are
trying to learn, the activities that get you there, your notes, and the record of your attempts.

## This copy is a starting point, and then it is yours

You clone this repository during first-day setup. In week 2 you publish it as **your own private
repository**, and from then on it is yours: you commit to it, you push to it, and it is backed
up somewhere other than your laptop.

It keeps a second remote called `upstream`, pointing back here, so that anything the instructor
adds later reaches you with `git pull upstream main`.

## Who can see it

**Nobody but you, by default.** Your copy is private, and the teaching team is not added to it.
That is deliberate: this is a record of learning, not something you are graded on. Work you hand
in goes in the `assignments` repository instead, which the teaching team can read.

You may share this repository if you want to — with a study partner, say. That is your call. The
setup check will notice and ask you to confirm you meant to, because sharing it by accident is
easier than it sounds.

## What's here now

**One topic, ready to start: `commits-and-history-2026-09`.** That one ships with this
repository rather than being one you began, so its goals and its activities were written for
you in advance. Open its `goals.md` first: three sections near the top are deliberately blank,
and they are yours to fill in.

Every other topic gets created here by the workflows in
[course-materials](https://github.com/umsi212F2026/course-materials) when you start one. A
folder looks roughly like this:

    reading-bpmn-diagrams-2026-09/
      goals.md         what you want to be able to do, and what would count as getting there
      activities.md    the work that gets you there
      notes.md         yours; you write, the agent prompts
      evidence/        the record of attempts, kept by a program rather than by hand

You do not create these by hand, and you do not need to remember the layout. Ask to start a new
topic and you will be walked through it.
