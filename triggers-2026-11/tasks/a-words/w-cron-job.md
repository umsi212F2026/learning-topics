# Cron job

### catch-pick

A family's shared laptop has a cron job that copies the photos folder to an external drive at
1 a.m. every night.

In this workflow, what is the cron job?

1. A program running all day, waiting for 1 a.m.
2. A command started each time a photo is added
3. The list of photos waiting to be backed up
4. A command the system starts at 1 a.m.

### catch-missed

A runner has a cron job on his laptop that builds a summary of his week's training at 6 a.m.
every Monday. One Monday the laptop is turned off until 8. He says:

"That's fine. The cron job will see it missed 6 a.m. and run as soon as I turn the laptop on."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.
