A youth soccer club sends a notice to every family on a team when that team's field is closed, and
an AI agent writes and sends each notice.

**About this workflow:** The groundskeeper closes fields on the club's website a few times a month,
at no set times. Each notice must reach the team's families within half an hour. Nobody can sit
watching for closures all day, since they come at no set times. Every run, even one that finds no
new closure, has the agent reread every team's family list, which costs real money. The club
secretary checks the club's website each evening at 7 p.m. A parent volunteer designed the notice,
with the club's crest in one corner.
