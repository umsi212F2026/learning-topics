A small museum keeps a lost-and-found page on its website, and an AI agent writes the listing for
each item the staff find.

**About this workflow:** Found items are logged on the lost-and-found form a few a day, at no set
times. Each listing is needed within the hour. Every run, even one that finds no new item, has the
agent reread the whole lost-and-found log, which costs real money. The front desk opens at 10 a.m.
The lost-and-found page has a photo of the museum's front steps in the snow.
