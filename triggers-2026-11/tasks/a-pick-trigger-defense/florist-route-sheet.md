Rosa owns a neighborhood florist, and an AI agent on her laptop makes the delivery driver's route
sheet. Each morning the agent turns every order placed since the last sheet into one route sheet.

**About this workflow:** Customers place orders on the shop's website at all hours, including while
the laptop is off. The driver wants that morning's orders on the sheet. The laptop is turned off at
10 p.m. On most days the laptop is turned on at 6 a.m. On flower-market days the laptop is turned
on at 9 a.m. Rosa wants the route sheet as soon as the laptop is on. The route sheet is printed on
pink paper with the shop's name.

### q1

Which of these fits this workflow?

1. A schedule at 6:15 a.m. each morning, because on most days the laptop is turned on at 6 a.m.
2. Rosa starts it by hand when she turns the laptop on, because the route sheet is printed on pink paper with the shop's name.
3. Rosa starts it by hand when she turns the laptop on, because on flower-market days the laptop is turned on at 9 a.m.
4. A schedule at 9:30 p.m., just before shutdown, because the laptop is turned off at 10 p.m.
