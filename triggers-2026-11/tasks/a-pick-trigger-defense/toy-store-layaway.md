their paid-off gifts are ready to pick up.

**About this workflow:** The agent drafts each week's pickup reminders from the orders the owner
marks as paid off. The owner is away from Saturday evening until Tuesday. She marks the paid-off
orders once she is back, between 9 a.m. and 2 p.m. on Tuesday. She wants the reminders the moment
she has marked the orders. The reminders have a drawing of a teddy bear at the top.
