I manage an apartment building, and we text each resident when a package arrives for them. When the
delivery driver scans a package into our mailroom, the scanner sends out a notice naming the
resident, once, and keeps no copy. A small program on the building's office computer receives each
notice as the scanner sends it and hands it to an AI agent, which writes a short text telling the
resident which shelf the package is on. On most days the afternoon truck scans in about 150
packages in ten minutes, and the agent's provider takes only about five requests a minute. The agent
sends each text and keeps no copy of it. Residents like to hear the same afternoon, and a text
within an hour or so of the drop-off is fine. A resident who never gets a text may not know a
package is there. The mailroom is just inside the lobby door.
