I coordinate a group of volunteer translators, and people who need a letter or a form translated
ask for help through the request form on our website. A few requests come in a day, at all hours,
since many of the people asking work nights or live in other time zones. An AI agent on my laptop
reads each request, picks a volunteer who knows the language, and emails the person who asked with
that volunteer's name. My laptop is on from about 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays and turned off the
rest of the time. The website keeps every request that comes in while my laptop is off, with the
time it came in. The agent emails each match and keeps no copy of it. Everyone who writes is waiting
on a translation, but a reply on my next working morning is soon enough. Between them, our
volunteers speak fourteen languages.
