I'm a wedding photographer, and after each wedding I post the photos to a private gallery on my
website so the couple can pick the ones they want in their album. Couples send their picks through
the gallery whenever they get to it, a few sets a week, often late at night or on a weekend. The
gallery saves each couple's picks as a file in a shared online folder. An AI agent on my laptop
reads each picks file, puts the chosen photos in album order, and writes an edit list for the print
lab. The agent saves that edit list beside the couple's picks file, in the same folder. My laptop
is on from about 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays and turned off the rest of the time, and the agent
gets through any number of picks files in a few minutes. Every couple is waiting on an album, but
work starting on my next working day is soon enough. Most of my weddings are in the summer.
