My family runs a farm stand, and customers preorder boxes of berries through a form on the stand's
website. An AI agent on the farmhouse computer reads each preorder, picks a pickup time when we'll
have enough berries picked, and emails the customer. A confirmation within the hour is fine. The
preorder form can send a message to a web address each time a preorder comes in. Receiving those
messages means asking the friend who built our website, who is away on a sailing trip until the end
of the month, and preorders open this Saturday. A program can look at the form's list of preorders
in a moment, at no cost. The stand is painted red, with a hand-lettered sign at the end of our lane.

Should this workflow find out about each new preorder by polling or through a webhook? Say why.
