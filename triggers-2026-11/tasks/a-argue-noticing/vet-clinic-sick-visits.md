I manage a small vet clinic, and pet owners whose animal has suddenly fallen ill ask for a same-day
appointment through a form on our website. An AI agent on the front desk computer reads each
request, finds the earliest open slot with a vet, and texts the owner the time. A worried owner
wants that text within a minute or two, before they give up and drive to the emergency hospital
across town. The website can send a webhook each time a request comes in. Setting up the front
desk computer, which is never turned off, to take in those messages would take our computer person
a full day, and since she only comes in on Thursdays, it means booking and paying for an extra
visit. Each look at the requests means the agent reading every request on the form again, which
takes it several minutes and runs up the bill for the agent. Our clinic cat, Biscuit, sleeps on the
front desk most afternoons.

### q1

Should this workflow find out about each new appointment request by polling or through a webhook?
Say why. Answer in two or three sentences.
