I direct a youth band, and parents tell us when their child will miss a rehearsal by emailing the
band's shared mailbox. An AI agent on the band's office computer reads each absence note, adds the
player to the missing list for that rehearsal, and sends the parent a short thank-you. The section
leaders read the missing list each morning, so a note handled any time before the end of the day it
arrives is fine. The mailbox sends no notice when new mail arrives. A program can open it and look,
which takes a moment and costs nothing. Our players wear green and gold uniforms.
