I run a small print shop, and customers who need something printed the same day send us rush jobs
through the order page on our website. An AI agent on the shop's front computer reads each rush
job, works out a price and a pickup time, and emails them to the customer. Each rush job should be
handled after it comes in, not at set times, and customers want that email within a few minutes.
The order page can send a message to a web address each time a rush job comes in. My partner, who
looks after our computers, can set up the front computer, which is never turned off, to take in
those messages in an afternoon. The order page lets a program look at its list of orders at most
once an hour. Our shop's sign is a pelican holding a pencil.

### q1

Should this workflow find out about each new rush job by checking for new ones again and again, or
by being told each time one arrives? Say why. Answer in two or three sentences.
