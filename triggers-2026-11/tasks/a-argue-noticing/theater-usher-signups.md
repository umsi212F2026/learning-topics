I coordinate the volunteer ushers at a community theater, and volunteers sign up for performances
through a page on the theater's website, which keeps a list of every sign-up. An AI agent on the
box office computer reads each new sign-up, assigns the volunteer a door, and emails them their
instructions. A reply by the end of the day is fine. The website can send a notice each time
someone signs up, but notices need a web server to arrive at, and the box office computer doesn't
run one. The box office computer is connected to the internet and can fetch the list of sign-ups
from the website. Fetching the list is quick and costs nothing. The theater's lobby was once a
bank, and the old vault is now the coat room.
