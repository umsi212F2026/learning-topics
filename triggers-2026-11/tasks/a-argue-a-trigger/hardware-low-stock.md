I own a hardware store, and an AI agent on the computer in the back office tells me when an item
is running low, with a note of how many to bring out from the stockroom. The register keeps a
running count of every item, and an item runs low whenever a sale takes it down to its last three.
On a busy day that happens ten or fifteen times, at no set times. I want to know within half an
hour, because contractors come in and clear out the last few boxes of screws or fittings fast.
Nobody at the counter can keep an eye on the counts all day, since items run low at no set times.
Each time the agent runs, it reads through the counts for all twenty thousand items in the store
and the supplier's whole price list, which costs real money even when nothing has run low. My
grandfather opened the store in 1962.
