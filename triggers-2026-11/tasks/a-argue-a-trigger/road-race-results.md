I direct the road races our town's recreation department puts on. An AI agent on the laptop I
bring to the finish line builds the results page for each race from the finish times the timing
clock records. We put on four or five races a year, and the dates change from year to year,
depending on when the parks are free. On race day, the last runner can finish anywhere from noon
to two in the afternoon, depending on the course and the weather. Once the last runner is in, I
upload the finish times from the timing clock, and the agent builds the page from them. I read the
page over as soon as it is built, there at the finish line, before it goes on the department's
website. Our biggest race is a 10K along the river.
