I represent the east side of town on the city council. An AI agent on the computer in my council
office writes and sends a reply to each constituent who writes in, naming the city office that
handles their problem. Constituents write through the contact form on my council page, a few a day,
at no set times. Someone writing about a broken streetlight or a blocked drain should have that
reply within the hour. Nobody on my staff can sit watching for new messages all day, since they
come at no set times. Each time the agent runs, it rereads my whole constituent file to see who has
written before, which costs real money even when no new message has come in. We started using the
agent last spring.
