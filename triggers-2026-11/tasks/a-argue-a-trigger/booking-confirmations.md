I run a six-room bed-and-breakfast, and an AI agent on the computer at our front desk writes and
sends each guest's booking confirmation, with their dates, their room and the deposit they owe.
Bookings come in through the booking form on our website, usually three or four a day, at any hour,
and I can never guess when the next one will arrive. Guests who don't hear from us within the hour
tend to phone to ask whether the booking went through, and some book somewhere else instead. The
front-desk computer is never turned off. Each time the agent runs, it reads through every booking
on our calendar to find the ones it hasn't confirmed yet, which takes a few minutes and costs a
little in agent fees, even when nothing is new. I'm the only one here most days, and between
breakfast, laundry and check-ins I forget anything I'd have to start by hand. The booking form can
send a message to a web address each time someone books, but taking in those messages means my son
setting something up on the front-desk computer, and he only comes by on Sundays and grumbles about
computer chores. The confirmation email has a photo of our garden at the top.
