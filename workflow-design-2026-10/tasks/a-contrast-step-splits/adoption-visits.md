A pet shelter lets people who are thinking of adopting come in to meet an animal first, and
arranges these visits with a weekly workflow. About 30 people ask for a visit each week.

1. Gather the week's visit requests from the shelter's website into one list, each with the
   person's name, the animal they want to meet and the times they can come. (a program)
2. Set aside the requests for animals already adopted and from anyone under 18, by the shelter's
   rules, give each remaining request a half-hour slot within the times the person can come, with
   no two visits to the same animal at once, and write each visitor a short, welcoming email with
   their slot and what to expect. (an AI agent)
3. After each visit, write a few notes on how the visitor and the animal got along and whether to
   recommend the adoption, then pull each recommended visit into a follow-up list, and add each
   visitor's phone number from their request. (the volunteers who run the visits, then an AI agent)
4. Call each visitor on the follow-up list to arrange the adoption. (the shelter's adoption
   coordinator)

Some of these steps do more than one step's work.

### q1

Talia, Umar and Vera each propose splitting step 2 into two steps.

1. Talia: split after the agent gives each request a slot, and keep the list of visits, one line
   per visitor with animal, day and time.
2. Umar: split after the agent gives each request a slot, and pass the slots straight on to the
   emails without storing them.
3. Vera: split after the agent gives each request a slot, and keep a count of the visits booked for
   each animal.

Which proposal is the best division of step 2?

1. Talia's
2. Umar's
3. Vera's

### q2

Wes, Ximena and Yusuf each propose splitting step 3 into two steps.

1. Wes: split after the agent pulls the recommended visits into a follow-up list, and keep the
   week's visit list from step 2.
2. Ximena: split after the volunteers write their notes, and keep each visit's notes, with the
   volunteer's recommendation.
3. Yusuf: split after the agent pulls the recommended visits into a follow-up list, and keep that
   follow-up list of recommended visitors.

Which proposal is the best division of step 3?

1. Wes's
2. Ximena's
3. Yusuf's
