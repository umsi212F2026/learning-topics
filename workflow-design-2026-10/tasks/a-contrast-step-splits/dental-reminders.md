A dental office with three dentists reminds its patients of their appointments the evening
before, with a workflow it runs every afternoon. About 40 patients have appointments on a
typical day.

1. Copy the next day's appointments from the office's schedule into one list. (a program)
2. Pick out from each patient's record whether they want a text or an email, make a sending list
   with each appointment's time, dentist and that choice, then write each patient a short,
   friendly reminder saying when to come and what to bring. (an AI agent)
3. Send each reminder by text or email, as the sending list says. (a program)
4. Read the replies that come back overnight and mark each one confirmed, cancelled or asking for
   a new time, then choose an open slot to offer each patient asking for a new time, and choose a
   patient from the waiting list for each cancelled slot. (an AI agent, then the front-desk
   manager)
5. Text each patient the slot they are offered. (a program)

Some of these steps do more than one step's work.

### q1

Ana, Ben and Chloe each propose splitting step 2 into two steps.

1. Ana: split after the agent makes the sending list, and keep that list, one line per
   appointment with its time, dentist and text or email.
2. Ben: split after the agent makes the sending list, and keep the next day's appointments copied
   in step 1.
3. Chloe: split after the agent makes the sending list, and keep a count of how many patients
   chose text and how many chose email.

Which proposal is the best division of step 2?

1. Ana's
2. Ben's
3. Chloe's

### q2

Daniel, Elena and Farah each propose splitting step 4 into two steps.

1. Daniel: split after the manager chooses slots for patients asking for a new time, and keep the
   list of slots offered to each of them.
2. Elena: split after the manager chooses slots for patients asking for a new time, and pass those
   choices straight on without storing them.
3. Farah: split after the agent marks the replies, and keep the list of replies, each marked
   confirmed, cancelled or new time.

Which proposal is the best division of step 4?

1. Daniel's
2. Elena's
3. Farah's
