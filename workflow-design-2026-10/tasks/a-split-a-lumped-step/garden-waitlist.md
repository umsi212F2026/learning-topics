the plots that gardeners have given up. About 60 households are on the waitlist, and two to six
plots open up in a month.

1. Gather the plots given up in the past month into one list of plot numbers, each with its size.
   (a program)

   and decide which households to pass over this month, such as one that is moving in the spring,
   then match each open plot to the longest-waiting household not passed over, and fill in an
   offer email for each match with the household's name, the plot number and the date to reply
   by. (the plot coordinator, then a program)
3. Send each offer email to its household. (a program)
4. For each household that accepted its offer, write a two-sentence welcome to the new gardener
   for the garden's monthly newsletter, from what they wrote about themselves on the waitlist
   form, then set each welcome under its plot number and put them all in plot-number order. (an AI
   agent)
5. Send the newsletter to every member of the garden. (a program)
