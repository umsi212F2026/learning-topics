The referee coordinator of a youth soccer league runs this workflow every week of the season to
staff its Saturday games. About 20 games are played each Saturday, and about 35 referees are on
the league's list.

1. Gather each referee's Saturday availability from the league's sign-up form into one list, with
   the team their own child plays on, if any. (a program)
2. Assign two referees to each game from those available, so that no one is due at two fields at
   once and nobody referees a game their own child plays in, then print a card for each game
   showing its field, kickoff time and two referees, and write a short, courteous message to each
   game's two teams introducing their referees and asking parents to support them. (an AI agent)
3. Send each referee their games, and each team its message. (a program)
4. Read the field marshals' reports after the games and decide whether each referee is paid in
   full for each game, such as one who left at halftime because of lightning, then work out each
   referee's pay at $25 a game and add up the day's total for the league's bank transfer. (the
   referee coordinator, then a program)
5. Pay each referee the amount worked out. (a program)

Some of these steps do more than one step's work.
