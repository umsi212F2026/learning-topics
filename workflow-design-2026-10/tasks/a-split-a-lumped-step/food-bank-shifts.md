The volunteer coordinator at a neighborhood food bank runs this workflow every week to staff its
pantry shifts. About 80 volunteers sign up for about 25 shifts a week.

1. Gather the week's sign-ups from the volunteer form into one list of each volunteer's name and
   the shifts they can work. (a program)
2. Assign each volunteer to one of the shifts they can work so that no shift has fewer than three
   people, list the assignments by shift, and write each first-time volunteer a short, welcoming
   note about what to expect on their shift. (an AI agent)
3. Send each volunteer their shift, along with the note if they are new. (a program)
4. Read the shift leads' reports of who missed a shift and decide which absences to excuse, such
   as for illness or a family emergency, then count each volunteer's unexcused absences for the
   season and list everyone whose count is three or more. (the volunteer coordinator, then a
   program)
5. Phone each volunteer on that list to talk about whether their shifts still suit them. (the
   volunteer coordinator)

Some of these steps do more than one step's work.
