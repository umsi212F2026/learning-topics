yearly dues of $40. About 150 households belong to the association.

1. Make one list of member households from last year's roster, each with its street address. (a
   program)
2. Write a short, friendly letter from the treasurer's notes on last year's spending, telling
   members what their dues paid for and asking for this year's $40, then make a copy of the letter

   (an AI agent)
3. Mail the letters. (the treasurer)
4. Record each payment as it comes in against its household on the list. (a program)
5. List the households that have not paid by March 1, sort that list by street, then decide which
   of those households to visit with a reminder and which to leave alone this year, such as a
   neighbor who has just lost a job. (a program, then the treasurer)

Some of these steps do more than one step's work.
