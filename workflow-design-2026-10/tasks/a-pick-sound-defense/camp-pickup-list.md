Each question gives two people's defenses of the same choice for one step. Pick the sound one.

Pine Lake Day Camp has about 150 children each week of the summer. Its office manager runs this
workflow every week to keep the gate's list of which adults may pick up each child.

1. Collect the pickup forms that families fill in for the coming week. (a program)
2. Check the forms and approve the week's pickup list. (the office manager)
3. Enter each child's allowed adults from the approved list into the gate's check-in system.
   (who?)
4. Decide each family's later request to change who may pick up their child: allow it, refuse it,
   or call the family first. (who?)
5. Update the gate's check-in system with each decision and text the family. (a program)

**About step 3:** Its input is the pickup list the office manager approved at step 2, one line per
child with the adults allowed to collect them. A mistake here lets the wrong adult collect a child,
or turns away the right one, and nothing later catches it. It handles each child's name and each
adult's phone number. About four lines a week give a relationship instead of a name, such as "her
grandmother". The approved list is ready each Sunday evening.

**About step 4:** Its input is about five messages a week from parents, often about something the
camp's forms don't cover, such as a separated parent asking that the other be kept away. A wrong
decision could send a child home with an adult the family didn't want, and it reaches the gate
with no one checking it. The messages often describe a family's custody arrangement or a court
order. The camp director handles these on Monday mornings. The week's messages are gathered and
ready each Friday at 5 p.m.

### q1

Ruth and Jamal both think step 3 should be done by a program rather than an AI agent. Here is how
each puts it.

**Defense 1, Ruth's:** A program rather than an AI agent helps correctness, because it enters
exactly the adults on the approved list. It hurts timeliness, because it has to set aside the lines
that give a relationship instead of a name.

**Defense 2, Jamal's:** A program rather than an AI agent helps correctness, because it handles
each child's name and each adult's phone number. It hurts timeliness, because it has to set aside
the lines that give a relationship instead of a name.

Which defense is sound?

1. Ruth's
2. Jamal's

### q2

Elena and Sam both think step 4 should be done by a person rather than an AI agent. Here is how
each puts it.

**Defense 1, Elena's:** A person rather than an AI agent helps correctness, because a request to
keep a separated parent away needs a judgment the camp's forms don't settle. It hurts timeliness,
because no message can be decided before Friday at 5 p.m.

**Defense 2, Sam's:** A person rather than an AI agent helps correctness, because a request to keep
a separated parent away needs a judgment the camp's forms don't settle. It hurts timeliness,
because the director only gets to these on Monday mornings.

Which defense is sound?

1. Elena's
2. Sam's
