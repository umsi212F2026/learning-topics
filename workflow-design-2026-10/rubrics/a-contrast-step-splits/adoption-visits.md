Steps 2 and 3 are the lumped ones. Step 2 is `check-split`: the same agent does all three parts.
Setting requests aside by the shelter's rules and giving the rest their slots are both checked
against the requests and the rules (nobody turned away wrongly, every slot within the person's
times, no animal with two visits at once), so there is no boundary between them. The emails need
a person to read them for warmth and clarity, so the boundary goes before the emails. Step 3 is
`handoff-split`: the volunteers' notes and recommendations are judgment calls, checked only by
someone who saw the visit, while the agent's follow-up list and phone numbers are both checked
against the notes and the requests. Its join with no change is between pulling the recommended
visits and adding the phone numbers.
