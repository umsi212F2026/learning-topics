# deploy status

Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words, with nothing open in front of you.

### q1

Your backend runs on a host called Larkyard, and your agent reports that the deploy status of its
latest deploy is `live`. What is the difference between that and your app working?

### q2

A student pushed a change to their study-room finder, which has been working for weeks on
Larkyard. Their agent reports that the new deploy's status is `failed`. The student writes in the
team chat:

"Deploy status is failed, so the site is down for everyone until we fix it."

What is wrong with that?
