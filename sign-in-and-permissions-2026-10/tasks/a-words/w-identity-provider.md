# Identity provider

Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words, with nothing open in front of you.

Pantry is a recipe box for a campus cooking club. Its Express backend runs on Rivetbox, a server
host, and members sign in with their Google accounts. Members may edit or delete only the recipes
they posted.

### q1

What is the difference between Google's job as Pantry's identity provider and Rivetbox's job as
Pantry's host?

### q2

Pantry's backend has a `users` table with a row for each member: their Google id, name and
picture. A student says:

"Our identity provider is the `users` table, because that's where we look up who someone is."

What is wrong with that?

### q3

A student says:

"Google is Pantry's identity provider, so Google decides which members may edit which recipes."

What is wrong with that?
