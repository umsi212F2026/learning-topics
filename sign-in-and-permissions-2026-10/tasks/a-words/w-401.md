# 401

Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words, with nothing open in front of you.

Pantry is a recipe box for a campus cooking club, with sign-in through GitHub. Anyone signed in may
read every recipe, but only a recipe's author may delete it.

### q1

A student signs in as a second account and sends a request to delete a recipe the first account
posted. They say:

"The server should answer 401, since this user is unauthorized to delete someone else's recipe."

What is wrong with that?

### q2

A student signs out of Pantry and then sends a request to `/api/my-recipes`, which lists the
signed-in member's own recipes. The server answers 401. The student says:

"Sign-in must be broken: the server sent back 401."

What is wrong with that?
