# Rubric: free tier

If the learner misses a question here, set a DEFINE or INTERPRET move for free tier live, as help
(it is recorded as helped and doesn't count), then come back to a production question.

A free tier is what a vendor lets you use without paying, up to a limit. The confusable is a free
trial, which is free only for a while, or until a one-time credit runs out. "Free plan" and "hobby
plan" mean the same as free tier and are not confusables. Kettle is made up.

### q1

- **goal:** `w-free-tier`
- **move:** DISTINGUISH
- **answer:** A free tier has no end date: it stays free for as long as you stay under its limits.
  A free trial is free only for a set time, or until a one-time credit is used up, and then you pay
  or it stops, however little you used.
- **credit:** full for the difference that matters: what ends it, staying under a usage limit with
  no end date against a time or a one-time credit running out. Half for "a trial ends and a tier
  doesn't" with nothing on the tier's limit. None for an incidental difference alone, such as that
  a trial needs a card or that a tier is smaller, neither of which is always true.

### q2

- **goal:** `w-free-tier`
- **move:** CATCH
- **answer:** A free tier is free only up to a limit. With enough visitors the app passes it, and
  then the vendor pauses it, stops it, or starts charging, as its terms say.
- **credit:** full for naming that a free tier covers use only up to a limit, so many visitors can
  pass it. Half for "there will be limits" without saying that the number of visitors is what
  would pass them. None for a different quibble alone, such as that the app might sleep or that
  Kettle might end its free tier.
- **tutor note:** if the learner says Kettle might withdraw the free tier, agree that it could, and
  ask what the tier's own terms would say about a great many visitors.
