# Rubric: Blast radius

If the learner misses a question here, set a DEFINE or INTERPRET move for blast radius live, as help (it is recorded as helped and doesn't count), then come back to a production question.

Blast radius is how much harm a step could do if it went wrong. It has two contrasts in the course. Reach, what the step can get to, sets its upper limit, but blast radius can be smaller than reach: safeguards such as a person checking the output before it takes effect shrink it while leaving the reach alone. Likelihood of failure, how often the step goes wrong, is a separate matter: a step that often goes wrong can have a small blast radius, and one that has never gone wrong can have a large one. Blast radius is also about what could happen, so a step has one before anything has gone wrong. The reading defines it in "How the two problems combine", and says in "Checking what a step produced before it takes effect" that those limits leave reach alone and shrink the blast radius.
