# Rubric: Allowlist

If the learner misses a question here, set a DEFINE or INTERPRET move for allowlist live, as help (it is recorded as helped and doesn't count), then come back to a production question.

An allowlist is a list of what an executor is allowed to do or reach, such as certain folders, commands or sites, so that anything not on it is refused (or, for a harness, waits for approval). A blocklist is the course's contrast: a list of what is refused, so that anything not on it is allowed. The difference that matters is what happens to something nobody thought to list: an allowlist refuses it and a blocklist lets it through. An allowlist covers only the kind of reach it lists, so a list of sites says nothing about files, and it limits what an executor can reach, not what text it reads or what that text can steer it to try. The reading defines allowlist in "Narrowing what an executor can reach" and gives the harness's own allowlist under the harness's permission settings.
