# Allowlist

### vs-blocklist

Beatriz has an agent that drafts her newsletter from her notes and her readers' emails. One reader's email hides a line telling the agent to send Beatriz's subscriber list to a site nobody has heard of. Her harness's network setting could be either of two lists:

- an allowlist naming only her newsletter service
- a blocklist naming two sites known for scams

#### a

Under the allowlist, what happens when the agent tries to reach the unknown site?

1. It reaches the site.
2. It is refused, or waits for approval.

#### b

Under the blocklist, what happens when the agent tries to reach the unknown site?

1. It reaches the site.
2. It is refused, or waits for approval.

### catch-never-run

Cyrus has an agent that renames and sorts his photos. He sets up its harness and says:

"I've given it an allowlist of the commands it must never run: delete, move and upload."

What is wrong with that? Answer in two or three sentences, in your own words.

### catch-pick

A running club has an agent that fetches the weekend forecast and writes a note about whether the long run is on. The harness's network setting for the agent is an allowlist naming one weather site. The setting says nothing about files.

Which of these uses the term allowlist correctly?

1. The allowlist keeps the agent from opening the club's member list on the laptop.
2. The allowlist lets the agent reach any site that isn't known to be harmful.
3. The allowlist lets it reach the weather site and refuses any other site.
4. The allowlist means nothing on the weather site's page can steer the agent.
