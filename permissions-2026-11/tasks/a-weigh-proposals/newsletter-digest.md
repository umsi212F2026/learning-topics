Sofia subscribes to more newsletters than she can keep up with, and one step of her workflow gathers them for a digest she reads on Sundays.

- Every Saturday morning the collector, a program Sofia wrote, signs in to her email account, finds the week's newsletters, and saves a copy of each into a folder called newsletters.
  - It holds a token for her email account that lets its holder read, send and delete her mail.
  - Her home folder holds the collector, the newsletters folder, her photos and her tax returns.

The harm Sofia is worried about: a mistake in the collector's code makes it delete the mail in her inbox.

Sofia asks her agent what limits could reduce the risk of that harm.

## Framing

The agent proposes several limits, and each question below gives one.

Proposal: 'I can replace the collector's token with a scoped token that only reads her mail.'
