Pilar is a college student who buys her textbooks used, and one step of her workflow looks for this term's books on a used-textbook marketplace.

- Each evening the textbook agent, following a skill file Pilar wrote, fetches the new listings from a used-textbook marketplace, reads each one and her book list, which she typed, sends the seller of each book on her list a message through the marketplace asking whether it is still for sale, and writes those listings into a file called leads.
  - On the marketplace, any student with an account can list a book for sale.
  - Her home folder holds the skill file, the book list, the leads file and a coursework folder with her essays and lab reports for the term.
  - It holds a token for her marketplace account that reads listings, sends messages and buys books with her saved card.

The harm Pilar is worried about: the agent deletes the essays and lab reports in her coursework folder.

Her agent proposes: 'I can swap the textbook agent's token for a scoped token that reads listings and sends messages, and that can't buy anything.'
